The Right Software

Software that we provide to you.

CCH Tagetik

CCH Tagetik is a process performance management software for the Office of Finance. Its integrated financial planning, budgeting, forecasting, consolidation and reporting are connected in one mapped platform with CCH Tagetik. Your financial processes can be mapped from operational planning to external reporting with one software solution.

Sturmis365

The corporate disclosure software solution for your annual reports, financial and sustainability reports, Sturmis365 is a state-of-the-art software tool to streamline report generation. The software enables pre-system-independent, dynamic integration of business figures into your Word and PowerPoint reports.
